🛍️ How to Order & Check Out

  1. Pick a sticker from any category below and choose its options.
  2. Click “Add to Cart”. You can add as many different stickers as you want.
  3. Click the pink 🛒 Cart button at the bottom-right to review everything and see your total.
  4. Click “Proceed to Checkout”, then enter your name, email, and any notes needed for your order.
  5. Click “Place Order”. Your order is sent to CBK's shop email through the checkout form.
  6. You should see “Order Placed!” when the email submission succeeds. If something goes wrong, an error message will appear instead.

💡 Important: Adding an item to your cart does NOT send the order yet. The order is sent only after you complete checkout and click Place Order.
